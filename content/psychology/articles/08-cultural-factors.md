## 1 | Explain | 6

*Candidates are asked to explain the impact that culture has on conformity, using research evidence.*

Where candidates only provide descriptions of cultural factors, **a maximum of 3 marks** can be awarded.

Responses may include:

- culture is the ideas, customs and social behaviour of a particular people or society **(1)**
- collectivist cultures, such as China, Korea and Japan, emphasize family and work group goals above individual needs or desires **(1)**
- collectivist cultures conform more because non-conformity is more strongly associated with deviance (Kim and Markus, 1999) **(1)**
- individualistic cultures (USA, UK, Italy) view themselves as separate from others and define themselves based on personal traits **(1)**, so people are in general less likely to conform **(1)**
- average conformity rates are between 25% and 58% in collectivist cultures and between 14% and 39% in individualist cultures (Smith and Bond, 1993) **(1)**
- Belgians have the lowest level of conformity around the world (14%), while Fiji is at 58% **(1)**
- Asch studied males in 1950s America, during the Cold War, when Americans were encouraged to be conformist **(1)**, so his findings may not be generalised to all cultures **(1)**
