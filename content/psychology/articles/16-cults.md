## 1 (a) | Describe | 6

*Candidates are asked to describe what a cult is and how one can be identified.*

Responses may include:

- a cult is a group with beliefs and behaviours considered abnormal to society **(1)**
- members tend to have extreme or unusual views and usually isolate themselves from society, which can cause harm **(1)**
- Hassan (2012) identified behaviour control: regulating where members live, eat and sleep **(1)**
- information control: deception and lies, minimising access to non-cult sources **(1)**
- thought control: members have to accept the cult's view of reality as truth ("us versus them") **(1)**
- emotional control: members are taught to block out homesickness and doubt, with strong promotion of guilt and fear **(1)**

## 1 (b) | Describe | 6

*Candidates are asked to describe an example of a cult.*

Responses may include:

- Jim Jones presented himself as a minister and charity worker, running soup kitchens and food banks **(1)**
- he targeted ethnic minorities and spent more and more time with victims' families **(1)**
- he claimed the USA was at risk of nuclear war and persuaded hundreds to move to a South American commune, Jonestown **(1)**
- on arrival, members had to work long hours in the fields, and many became ill **(1)**
- he convinced the cult that if they died together, they would all go to paradise **(1)**
- members drank poisoned "kool-aid", and 900 people died **(1)**

## 1 (c) | Explain | 6

*Candidates are asked to explain how cults and manipulators control people.*

Manipulators include those in abusive relationships, salespeople and politicians.

Responses may include:

- targeting vulnerable people often isolated from family, religion or a support network **(1)**
- using people's weaknesses against them **(1)**
- brainwashing techniques **(1)**
- isolating them from friends and family, and from outside information **(1)**
- guilt and self-blame to encourage compliance: "Think of everything I've done for you" **(1)**
- fear and intimidation to encourage obedience: "You would be nothing without me" **(1)**
